A presidential campaign simulator

Run for
Office

Fifty-one contests. Five thousand one hundred simulated voters. Two hundred and seventy electoral votes. Write a biography, take five positions, spend what you have, and find out what the country thinks of you.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
1

The race

Every candidate is a real competitor in the simulation. Extra candidates split the electorate, and it becomes much easier for nobody to reach 270.

Each round you receive money and spend it. Polling is published at the end of every round.

2

The field

Mark a candidate as You to control them yourself. Two or more human candidates play hot-seat, passing the device between turns. Everyone else is run by the computer.

3

Who are you?

Write the biography the country will hear. The analyst reads it for occupation, place, specificity and self-inflicted wounds, and converts that into where you start with each slice of the electorate — before you have said a word about policy.

0 words
4

Your platform

Five issues were drawn for this election. Place yourself on each from −3 to +3. Voters hold their own position on every one, and they vote for whichever candidate points most nearly the same direction they do.
